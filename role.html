Co-Founder Opportunity

Help us build clean air infrastructure before the next pandemic.

Tidal Air is building fleet-managed air purification infrastructure for the 3+ million classrooms in the US, with a larger mission to make clean air permanent building infrastructure before the next pandemic.

Working prototypes are deployed and the pre-seed is being led by Halcyon Futures with Geoff Ralston (former YC president) and Matt Rogers (Nest co-founder) as angels.

We're looking for a non-technical co-founder to own school and district relationships, pilot execution, and sales from the ground up. Boston-based and startup experience preferred. If this sounds interesting please read on to learn more!

Who we're looking for.

This is a non-technical co-founder role. The external side of the company is yours to own: school and district relationships, getting the first pilots off the ground, and building the sales motion from scratch.

Early stage means the product, the customer, and the playbook are all still being figured out at the same time. The right person is energized by that rather than waiting for things to stabilize. Low ego and resilience matter a lot here. The work is hands-on and unglamorous in the early days, and the ability to update quickly when something is not working is as important as getting things right the first time.

We are looking for someone sharp and fast to learn with genuine curiosity about a new space. No background in air quality needed. What matters is real ownership over what you build and caring about what you are building.

Boston-based and startup experience preferred. If this sounds like you, or if you know someone it might, reach out directly.
