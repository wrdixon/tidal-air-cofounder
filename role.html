Co-founder seat

If you join, you own a significant piece of this company and everything that implies: the wins, the hard stretches, the decisions made with incomplete information, and the responsibility for whether this works. I'm looking for a true partner.

What the work looks like right now

Working prototypes are deployed and I'm preparing to run the first school pilots. These are early hardware going in front of real schools to learn what actually works, what teachers and facilities staff respond to, and what to build next. The technical foundation is solid enough to start learning. Everything on the outside of the company still needs to be built. That's the job.

Figure out the sales motion. I have a strong hypothesis about who buys this and why, but the real answers come from being in front of districts, watching what resonates, and updating quickly. You'll build that playbook from scratch.

Build the funding playbook for districts. Tidal Air isn't a line item most school budgets have room for. Part of the job is understanding what funding mechanisms actually exist, grants, state allocations, biosecurity-focused funders, and being able to hand a district a clear path to yes. This is BD, but it directly enables sales.

Lay the groundwork for distribution. At some point direct sales doesn't scale. The right installers, facilities contractors, or channel partners are out there. You won't build that in the first year, but you'll start.

Everything else. The work won't stay inside these lines, and some weeks the job is just figuring out what the job is. The ability to context-switch and stay useful when the problem changes is as important as anything on this list.

Who fits

I'm looking for someone energized by ambiguity rather than slowed down by it. Low ego. Resilient. Willing to be wrong quickly and update. The work in the early days is hands-on and unglamorous, and the ability to stay close to it, not just strategize about it, matters a lot.

Genuine curiosity about a new space and real ownership over what you build matter more than domain expertise. No background in air quality needed.

Boston-based and startup experience preferred. Neither is a requirement.
