Co-Founder Opportunity

Come build clean air infrastructure for every building in the country.

The opportunity: Biosecurity risks from rapidly improving AI and synthetic biology are growing quickly. Organizations including the OpenAI Foundation, Coefficient Giving, and ARPA-H are pouring hundreds of millions per year into preparing for what's coming, and recognize indoor air filtration as one of the most practical defenses against respiratory disease transmission at scale.

The indoor air purifier market is already $13 billion, larger now than at any point during the pandemic, driven by research linking clean air to attendance, cognitive performance, and health. Standards and state laws requiring verified air quality in occupied buildings are forming fast, and the market is growing at 7% annually.

As biosecurity risk becomes a mainstream concern, indoor air purification will move from nice-to-have, to expected or even mandated infrastructure in schools, offices, hospitals, and public spaces. The market won't just grow, it will radically transform.

But the products needed to make that happen don't exist. Everything available today is a consumer appliance. No fleet management, no verified outcomes, no way to deploy and maintain clean air across thousands of rooms from a single platform. The enterprise infrastructure layer that this market requires has not been built.

We're building it.

Where we are: Working prototypes deployed with live sensor data. Pre-seed being led by Halcyon Futures with Geoff Ralston (former Y Combinator president) and Matt Rogers (Nest co-founder) as angels. Preparing to run our first school pilots.

What we're looking for: A co-founder with a large equity stake to build the business side from the ground up: sales, district relationships, funding playbooks, and everything it takes to put this in every school in the country and beyond.
