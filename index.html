Co-Founder Opportunity

Help build clean air infrastructure before the next pandemic.

Tidal Air is building fleet-managed air purification infrastructure for the 3+ million classrooms in the US, with a larger mission to make clean air permanent building infrastructure before the next pandemic.

Working prototypes are deployed and the pre-seed is being led by Halcyon Futures with Geoff Ralston (former YC president) and Matt Rogers (Nest co-founder) as angels.

We're looking for a non-technical co-founder to own school and district relationships, pilot execution, and sales from the ground up. Boston-based and startup experience preferred. Learn more about the company or the role.
